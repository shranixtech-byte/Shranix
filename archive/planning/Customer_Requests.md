## Request Management Process

1. **Collect** — Customer submits request via support or account manager.
2. **Triage** — Product team evaluates feasibility, priority, and alignment.
3. **Estimate** — Development effort estimation (T-shirt sizing: S, M, L, XL).
4. **Schedule** — Added to roadmap or backlog.
5. **Communicate** — Customer notified of decision and timeline.
