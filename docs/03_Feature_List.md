## Detailed Feature Breakdown

### 1. Master Data Management

- Company/Branch setup
- Item/Product master with categories
- Unit of measure management
- Vendor/Supplier master
- Customer master
- Employee master
- Price list management
- Warehouse/Location master
- Tax code and rate configuration

### 2. Inventory Management

- Stock receipt and issuance
- Stock transfer between warehouses
- Stock adjustment (damage, loss, write-off)
- Batch/lot tracking
- Expiry date management
- Low stock alerts
- Inventory valuation (FIFO, weighted average)
- Barcode scanning support

### 3. Purchasing & Procurement

- Purchase order creation
- Purchase receipt against PO
- Direct purchase entry
- Purchase return processing
- Vendor price comparison
- Reorder level alerts
- Purchase approval workflow

### 4. Sales & Order Management

- Sales order entry
- Invoice generation
- Delivery challan management
- Sales return processing
- Credit note / Debit note
- Discount and scheme management
- Customer price tier management

### 5. Accounts & Finance

- Chart of accounts
- General ledger
- Accounts payable (vendor bills)
- Accounts receivable (customer payments)
- Day book / Cash book / Bank book
- Profit & Loss statement
- Balance sheet
- Trial balance
- Cash flow statement

### 6. User & Role Management

- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Granular permission settings
- User activity logging
- Session management
- Password policy enforcement

### 7. Taxation (GST - India Focus)

- GST invoice format
- GST return data export (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B)
- Input tax credit tracking
- E-way bill integration (future)

### 8. Reporting & Analytics

- Inventory reports (stock summary, movement, aging)
- Sales reports (daily, monthly, comparative)
- Purchase reports
- Accounts reports (ledger, trial balance, P&L)
- Custom report builder
- Export to PDF / Excel

### 9. Dashboard

- Key metrics: today's sales, outstanding, stock alerts
- Visual charts: bar, line, pie for trends
- Quick action buttons for common tasks
- Role-specific dashboard views
